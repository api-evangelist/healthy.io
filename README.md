Healthy.io is a digital-health company that turns an ordinary smartphone camera into a clinical-grade measurement device, using colorimetric analysis, computer vision and machine learning trained on more than a million tagged color-spectrum images. Its Minuteful product line covers home urinalysis for chronic kidney disease (Minuteful Kidney, FDA 510(k) cleared and CE marked), urinary tract infection testing (Minuteful UTI), a ten-parameter urinalysis panel (Minuteful 10), and digitized chronic wound assessment (Minuteful for Wound). The company sells to health systems, provider groups and payers - including Medicare Advantage and nonprofit health plans - rather than to developers, and states that its technology integrates into most EMRs via SMART on FHIR. Healthy.io operates from Boston, London and Tel Aviv, and publishes a detailed public trust center covering security, privacy, availability and compliance, including a coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Healthy.io: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
